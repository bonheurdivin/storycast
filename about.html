Our mission

Real voices. Real stories. Shared understanding.

StoryCast is a digital storytelling platform concept that shares human experiences, celebrates cultural heritage, and highlights personal growth and positive change. Our mission is to help people discover perspectives beyond their own and build greater empathy through authentic stories from Rwanda and around the world.

Discover stories

Why StoryCast exists

Stories bring different worlds closer.

Behind every experience is a perspective worth understanding. StoryCast is a digital storytelling concept designed to share human-interest stories about culture, personal growth, community, and innovation in Rwanda and beyond.

We want visitors to approach each story with curiosity, question their assumptions, and discover perspectives beyond their own everyday experiences.

Our name reflects the idea of sharing stories with a wider audience through accessible digital experiences.

What guides us

Our storytelling principles.

These principles guide how StoryCast content should be researched, presented, and experienced.

01

Respect every voice

Represent people's experiences thoughtfully, seek informed consent, and avoid presenting assumptions as facts.

02

Make stories accessible

Provide clear structure, readable text, keyboard access, and alternatives for audio and visual information.

03

Stay curious

Encourage visitors to learn from different perspectives and consider how individual experiences connect to society.

Access for everyone

Stories should be easier to experience.

StoryCast incorporates accessibility features to make its content easier to navigate and experience. These include keyboard navigation, visible focus indicators, readable page structure, responsive layouts, video captions, and a text transcript for the featured story.

Aa

Readable content

Semantic headings, clear page structure, readable typography, and comfortable line spacing help visitors understand and navigate the content.

↹

Keyboard navigation

Visitors can navigate links using a keyboard and identify the currently focused element through visible focus styles. A skip link provides a shortcut to the main content.

CC

Media alternatives

The featured video includes an English caption track and a separate text transcript, giving visitors alternative ways to access its spoken content.

↗

Responsive layouts

Responsive CSS layouts adapt the page to mobile phones, tablets, and desktop screens to help keep content accessible across different viewport sizes.

A work in progress

Building with accessibility in mind.

StoryCast is a student web-development project. Its accessibility features will be tested during development; this description is not a claim of formal WCAG certification.
